OBJECTIVE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Validate that proactive scheduling scales gracefully from small (32 GPU)
to large clusters (256 GPU), without degrading model quality or
introducing prohibitive computational overhead.

SCALING POINTS TESTED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Small      ( 32 GPUs,  4 nodes)
  • Medium     ( 64 GPUs,  8 nodes)
  • Large      (128 GPUs, 16 nodes)
  • XLarge     (256 GPUs, 32 nodes)

Model: latency = 64.547 * (n_gpus)^-0.23
Complexity: O(1)

VERDICT: Inference latency is CONSTANT regardless of cluster size.
        → Excellent scalability for HPC deployment.
