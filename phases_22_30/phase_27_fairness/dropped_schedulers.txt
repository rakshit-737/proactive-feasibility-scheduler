Phase 27: schedulers EXCLUDED from fairness/SLA analysis
========================================================
No real benchmark data exists in this repository for the
following schedulers; rather than inventing numbers they are
omitted from fairness_metrics.csv and sla_compliance.csv:

  - slurm_backfill (SLURM Backfill)
  - kubernetes_qos (Kubernetes QoS)
  - yarn_fifo (Yarn FIFO)

To include them, implement the schedulers in the simulation
pipeline (see 04_scheduler/) and regenerate the benchmark CSVs.
