======================================================================
CONCLUSION
======================================================================
In the paired 40-run benchmark, the proactive scheduler reduces
mean wait vs. FIFO by 7.71% on average (p = 1.45e-06).
In the multi-scheduler benchmark it outperforms Priority, EASY backfill + predicted-wait scan, EASY backfill (user estimates), EASY backfill (oracle), Conservative backfill, FIFO (head-blocking) on mean-wait reduction.
However, it is outperformed on mean wait by Shortest Job First (oracle), SJF (user estimates), Preemptive SRPT (+1 tick overhead), Highest Response Ratio Next, Smallest requested size first (no ML), Neural Network (MLP),
and its wait-time Gini coefficient is higher (less fair) than FIFO's.
These comparisons cover only the schedulers implemented in this
project's simulator; claims about external production schedulers
(SLURM, Kubernetes, Yarn) would require dedicated implementations.
